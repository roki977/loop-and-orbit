## SD-001 — Research theme: general-purpose agents through strategic interaction

- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision (proposed):** Study general-purpose agents' decision-making through strategic interaction, rather than asking which model is stronger. Theme: *Understanding General-Purpose Agents Through Strategic Interaction*. Five candidate directions (A–E); preferred main-line candidate: A1 cross-game transfer (conditions and boundaries), with A2 adaptation cost as an evaluation dimension and A3 memory as a mechanism variable. Pilot LO-EXP-001: H1 transfer helps, H2 negative transfer; 4 games (Prisoner's Dilemma, Stag Hunt, Chicken, Battle of the Sexes); 5 experience conditions (G0–G4); metrics Adaptation Speed and Negative Transfer Rate.
- **Context:** Proposed by the AI research commander (ChatGPT) in the Loop & Orbit planning conversation. Warns against treating "general beats specialized" as an established hypothesis — it must be broken into falsifiable questions.
- **Status:** proposed — pending human decision. Novelty Gate check required before adoption. Independent pre-screen (2026-11-17) verdict: MODIFY — narrow to in-context, memory-mediated transfer in repeated 2×2 games, with structural similarity as a continuous variable.
