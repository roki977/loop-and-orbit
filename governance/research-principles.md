# Research Principles

1. **Originality over superficial complexity.** A clear, well-posed question beats an impressive-looking setup.
2. **Rigor over impressive results.** Methods, controls, and statistics matter more than a striking number.
3. **Never claim novelty without checking prior work.** Search the closest work first; the Novelty Gate (`research/novelty-assessment.md`) is mandatory.
4. **Never fabricate papers, citations, results, or benchmarks.** Every citation must be verifiable.
5. **Distinguish facts, hypotheses, interpretations, and recommendations.** Keep them labelled in every document.
6. **Prefer low-cost, reproducible experiments.** API + Python + open-source game environments.
7. **Report negative and inconclusive results honestly.** A null result is a finding, not a failure.
8. **The goal is discovering what is actually true.** Not making the group look successful.

Long-term measure of the work: the ability to ask good questions, identify prior work, design rigorous experiments, and revise views when results disagree — not paper count, win rate, or readership.
